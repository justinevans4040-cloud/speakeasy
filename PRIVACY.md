# SpeakEasy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 3, 2026

SpeakEasy by ForgeFront is designed to provide private, on-device dictation and transcription.

## Microphone and transcription data

SpeakEasy uses microphone access only when the user starts dictation. Microphone audio is processed locally on the user's device. ForgeFront Systems does not receive, collect, transmit, sell, or share microphone recordings or transcript content.

Audio buffers used for transcription are temporary and are discarded after processing. SpeakEasy does not upload recordings or transcripts to ForgeFront Systems.

## Local storage

Transcripts, drafts, preferences, downloaded speech-recognition models, and application settings may be stored locally on the user's device. This information is not uploaded to ForgeFront Systems. Users may edit, export, copy, clear, or delete their locally stored information.

## Microsoft Store purchases

Optional purchases, subscriptions, and license verification are processed by the Microsoft Store. ForgeFront Systems does not receive or store payment-card information. Microsoft's handling of Microsoft account, purchase, and payment information is governed by Microsoft's privacy policy.

## Accounts, advertising, and tracking

SpeakEasy does not require a separate SpeakEasy account. SpeakEasy does not contain third-party advertising, advertising trackers, or ForgeFront Systems analytics.

## Data sharing

ForgeFront Systems does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information from SpeakEasy. Information that a user chooses to copy or export is controlled by the user and by any application or destination the user selects.

## Data deletion

Users may delete transcripts and other locally stored SpeakEasy data through the application's controls or by uninstalling the application.

## Children's privacy

SpeakEasy is not designed to collect personal information from children. ForgeFront Systems does not knowingly collect children's personal information through SpeakEasy.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SpeakEasy's functionality or legal requirements change. Updated versions will identify a revised effective date.
