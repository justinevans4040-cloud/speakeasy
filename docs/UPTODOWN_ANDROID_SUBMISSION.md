## Full description

SpeakEasy by ForgeFront is a private voice-to-text application built for fast, focused dictation without accounts, advertising, or unnecessary tracking.

Speak naturally into your device and SpeakEasy converts your speech into editable text using an on-device speech-recognition engine. Audio used for transcription is processed locally on your device rather than being sent to ForgeFront Systems.

SpeakEasy includes a free demonstration mode so you can explore the application before unlocking full functionality.

### Features

- Private on-device voice-to-text transcription
- Clean transcript editor
- Copy, save, edit, and export transcripts
- Automatic punctuation options
- Smart capitalization
- Optional filler-word removal
- Custom vocabulary and text replacements
- Multiple transcription quality settings
- Microphone selection and testing
- Noise suppression and echo cancellation controls
- Adjustable text size and accessibility options
- Automatic local draft saving
- TXT export
- No advertising
- No separate SpeakEasy account
- No ForgeFront Systems analytics or advertising trackers

### Demo mode

SpeakEasy is free to download and initially operates in demonstration mode. The demo allows users to explore the interface, settings, and capabilities before unlocking full access.

### Offline-focused

An internet connection may be required initially to download the selected speech-recognition model. Once the necessary model is available locally, transcription is designed to operate locally without continually sending speech to an online transcription service.

### Privacy

ForgeFront Systems does not receive, collect, sell, or share microphone recordings or transcript content. Transcripts, drafts, preferences, downloaded speech models, and application settings may be stored locally on the device.
